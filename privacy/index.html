Privacy Policy for Now Launcher

Now Launcher is provided by TIGERSTORM LIMITED (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what the Android app and this website process, what stays on your device, what is sent to other services, and the choices available to you.

Launcher settings and personalisation are mainly stored on your device. Online features, account services, purchases, diagnostics, and feedback also involve processing outside your device. We do not sell personal data. The app does not display third-party adverts.

1. Launcher Data and Device Permissions

Now Launcher uses the following information to provide its features. Optional permissions can be refused or revoked in Android settings; the related features may then be unavailable.

  • Installed apps and launcher activity: App names, package identifiers, icons, shortcuts, and available device profiles help us display and launch your apps. Records of apps opened through Now Launcher, including opening times, are stored locally to personalise suggestions. This is launcher activity, not a record of everything you do inside other apps.
  • Contacts: With permission, the app reads contact details and photos for search, favourites, and shortcuts. Favourite contact identifiers and names may be saved locally. The contact integration does not upload your address book to our servers.
  • Calendar: With permission, the app reads calendar and event details to display upcoming items. The calendar integration does not upload your calendar to our servers. Your calendar provider may separately sync your calendar.
  • Notification access and media: Access to media notifications and active media sessions supports playback information and controls. This integration processes notification and media content locally rather than sending it to our servers. Android's notification-access permission can allow access beyond the media information shown.
  • Content you create: Reminders, routines, custom cards, saved destinations, favourites, search history, settings, avatars, and customised icons are stored locally. Notifications and alarms are used to deliver timers and reminders.
  • Device controls: Features such as brightness, volume, Bluetooth, torch, and wallpaper use device settings and related permissions. Choosing an image or taking a photo for customisation may use Android's image picker or camera.

The current Google Play build has Health Connect access disabled and does not request Health Connect read permissions. Earlier versions that offer an activity card may read steps, distance, and calories burned after you grant access. That integration displays the information locally and does not upload health readings to our servers.

When you choose an action such as calling a contact or opening directions to an event, the relevant phone number, address, or destination is passed to the app or service that carries out the action. Its own privacy policy applies.

2. Search and Online Content

The app stores up to 50 recent searches locally to offer history suggestions. You can remove individual history entries in the app. When the app fetches online autocomplete suggestions, the text you type is sent to Google's suggestion service before you submit a web search. This can include text you enter while looking for a local app or contact. The service also receives connection information such as your IP address.

Submitting a search or opening a link sends the query or URL to the selected browser, Google Search, YouTube, assistant, or other chosen service. The launcher does not read your browser's browsing history.

Weather requests use wttr.in and include your chosen place name when supplied. A request without a place name allows the provider to use your IP address to estimate an area. Sports cards request fixture or race information from content services, including the team or fixture selection needed for the request. Online images and website icons are fetched from their hosts; requesting a website icon from Google sends the website address used for that icon. Website previews may also contact the website you enter.

These requests can happen when enabled cards refresh or images load, as well as when you tap an action. Their recipients can process request details and IP addresses under their own policies. Avoid entering sensitive information into online search or public feedback.

3. Optional Sign-In and Purchases

Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication provide optional account access. Signing in makes your Google account identifier, name, email address, and available profile details accessible to the authentication service and the app. We use account information to identify your account and link premium access; signing in does not automatically upload your launcher settings, contacts, or calendar.

Google Play processes payments, and RevenueCat manages purchase validation, subscriptions, restoration, and premium entitlements. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, purchase and subscription records, and related device or app information. If you sign in, your Firebase user identifier is linked to RevenueCat so purchases can be associated with your account. Billing services can also operate with an SDK-generated app user identifier when you are not signed in.

Now Launcher does not receive your full payment-card number or bank credentials. Purchase and entitlement information is available to us through our billing provider and is cached by the app as needed to manage access. Using the app without an account does not prevent billing or diagnostic services from initialising.

4. Diagnostics and Analytics

The app includes Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to investigate failures and understand app usage. These services may collect crash reports, error details, interaction or session events, app version, device model, operating system, installation or app-instance identifiers, and connection information. Analytics may also access an Android advertising identifier where available under your device settings and the service configuration. These identifiers and reports are not necessarily anonymous.

Diagnostic and analytics services can initialise automatically when the app starts, including when you are not signed in. Revoking contact or calendar permissions does not disable these services. We use diagnostics and analytics to improve reliability and understand usage; the presence of an advertising identifier does not mean the app shows adverts. Google explains its processing in the linked policies below.

5. Feedback and Support

In-app feedback, bug reports, and feature requests send the title, description, and category you provide through our feedback service to GitLab. These requests are publicly viewable. Device model, manufacturer, brand, display, and RAM details are included only if you select “Include device details”. Do not include passwords, private contact details, health information, or other confidential material in a public report.

For private support or a privacy request, email hello@tigerstorm.dev. We receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Contact us if you need personal information removed from a feedback request we control; copies made by others may remain.

6. Storage, Backups, and Retention

Local app data remains until you remove it, it is replaced or pruned by the app, or you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Removing Android permissions stops the related access but does not necessarily erase saved favourites, settings, or content you created.

Android backup and device transfer can copy app preferences and selected files, including search history, launcher activity, reminders, favourites, settings, avatars, and customised icons. Depending on your device and backup settings, copies can be held by Google or your device's backup provider and restored on reinstall or another device. Uninstalling or clearing storage does not by itself delete those backups. Use your device or backup provider's controls to manage them.

Account records remain while the account exists, subject to deletion requests. Purchase records, diagnostics, support messages, and public feedback have separate retention: the relevant service's settings and policies, the time needed to resolve support or reliability issues, and applicable accounting, legal, or dispute obligations determine how long they are retained. Public feedback can remain after you stop using the app. Contact us for information about a particular record or a deletion request.

We use Android's app storage protections and HTTPS for connections to our account, billing, diagnostic, and feedback services. Third-party services and websites have their own security practices. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

7. Service Providers and This Website

Services described in this policy include:

  • Google — sign-in, Google Play, search, online icons, and applicable Android backups.
  • Firebase — authentication, Crashlytics, and analytics.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • GitLab — public feedback requests.
  • wttr.in and the hosts of requested content, images, and websites — online feature requests.

This website loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts. Those requests send connection information, including your IP address, to Google. Visiting this website also sends your IP address, requested page, and browser information to the hosting service; these can appear in operational logs. The account-deletion page uses Firebase Authentication.

Providers may process information outside your country, including outside the UK or European Economic Area. Their processing locations, retention terms, and transfer safeguards are described in their privacy and service documentation. You can contact us for information about providers processing your data on our behalf.

8. Your Choices, Account Deletion, and Rights

  • Review or revoke optional permissions in Android settings, and disable optional cards in the app where available.
  • Use the launcher without Google sign-in, remove individual search-history entries, or clear local app data through Android settings.
  • Manage Android backups and advertising identifiers using the controls available on your device or Google account.
  • Delete your Firebase sign-in account using the app's “Delete Account” link or the account-deletion page. Use the same Google account you used in Now Launcher.

Account deletion, clearing local storage, removing a backup, and deleting public feedback are separate actions. Deleting the sign-in account does not automatically erase local launcher data, backups, public reports, or every record held by billing or diagnostic providers. Email us to request deletion of associated personal data we control and to discuss any records that must be retained. We may need to verify your identity.

Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it through Google Play subscriptions.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or receive a portable copy of your personal data. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us to exercise these rights or raise a concern.

You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can complain to your local data protection authority, including the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe this has happened, contact us so we can investigate and address it.
