Roadmap

What’s next for Now Launcher.

A look at where we want to go, and the small steps that have brought us here.

A direction, rather than a promise. We want to deliver everything on this roadmap, but it may not always be possible. Priorities can change, ideas may evolve, and some features may never arrive. Upcoming items have no confirmed release dates.

Last updated 4 October 2026

Looking ahead

Planned · timing to be confirmed

  1. More suggestion types

    Planned

    Explore more ways to bring useful information and actions to your home screen, with new suggestion types that earn their place in your day.

  2. Better battery efficiency

    Planned

    Reduce unnecessary background work and refreshes so suggestions stay useful while asking less of your battery.

  3. A smarter suggestion visibility cycle

    Planned

    Refine when suggestions appear, how long they remain visible and when they return, so the panel feels timely without becoming repetitive.

  4. A more useful Explore page

    Planned

    Improve how you discover features and learn what Now Launcher can do, with clearer guidance and an easier path to trying things out.

  5. Lifetime access

    Planned

    Update billing to support a lifetime purchase option, with clearer choices alongside recurring subscriptions.

  6. Lights Out dark mode

    Planned

    Add a deeper, near-black theme for a quieter home screen at night and for anyone who prefers a darker display.

  7. More robust app-closing animations

    Planned

    Improve the transition back to the home screen across different apps and navigation gestures, with more consistent motion and fewer visual interruptions.

Q1 2026

  1. Suggestions for everyday moments

    Completed

    Brought more of your day into one panel, with configurable suggestions and quick access to the information that matters.

    • Apps & contacts: useful starting points for opening an app or reaching someone.
    • Weather: current conditions and forecasts to help you plan your day.
    • Calendar: upcoming events, so your next commitment is easy to spot.
    • Travel: journey estimates for home and work, with location access enabled.
    • Media: playback information and controls for what you are listening to.
    • Bedtime: a timely nudge as your configured bedtime approaches.
    • Sports: football and Formula 1 updates, available in beta.

  2. Your own custom suggestions

    Completed

    Create personal suggestions and choose when they should appear, giving your routines a place on the home screen.
